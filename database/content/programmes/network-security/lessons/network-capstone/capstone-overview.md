## The capstone scenario

You will review the network of a fictional company that has grown quickly and now has a mostly flat network, a single perimeter firewall with years of accumulated rules, and remote staff connecting through a VPN that places them on the internal network.

## What you will produce

1. **Topology review**: identify zones that should exist, trust boundaries, and where critical systems sit.
2. **Weakness register**: architectural and configuration weaknesses with their risk.
3. **Traffic analysis**: findings from supplied, pre-extracted traffic evidence.
4. **Improvement plan**: a target segmentation design and a revised, least-privilege firewall policy.
5. **Technical security report**: findings, evidence, risk and prioritised recommendations.

## How it is assessed

The firewall policy and traffic analysis parts are validated automatically in labs. The written report is self-assessed against a checklist, so you finish with an artefact you can discuss in interviews (with the fictional scenario clearly labelled).

## Report checklist

- [ ] Current-state topology description and diagram
- [ ] Target zones and the flows each requires
- [ ] Firewall rules with owners and justifications
- [ ] Evidence-backed traffic findings
- [ ] Prioritised remediation roadmap with quick wins first
- [ ] Residual risks and assumptions

## Ground rules

All work uses supplied data and AMANIQ simulators. Do not scan or capture traffic on networks you do not own or have explicit written permission to assess.
